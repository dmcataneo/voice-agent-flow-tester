How it works

Hey! I built this because "just put a voice agent on the phones" sounds simple and the math is not. A bot that solves most calls is great. A bot that solves a few and annoys the rest can cost more than it saves.

There is no voice, no speech recognition and no AI model in here, and nothing to pay for. Every step of the flow is a probability you set, so you can see which ones move the cost and which ones barely matter.

It's a small experiment with made-up numbers, not a forecast for any real company or product.

The flow. The bot asks what the caller wants. If it does not understand, it tries again (up to the retry limit), then passes the caller to a person. If it understands, it solves the call or it does not. When it does not, callers either ask for a person (with the bot's notes, so the person is faster) or hang up. A few "solved" calls were not really solved, so the caller phones again and reaches a person.

Cost per resolved call. Bot minutes times the bot price, plus person minutes times the person price, plus a fixed cost for every call that ends unresolved, divided by the calls that actually ended resolved. Hang-ups cost money and resolve nothing.

People-only. The same calls, answered by a person from the start, who resolves the share you set.

Not covered. Waiting in a queue, peak hours, caller mood, repeat callers beyond the one above, setup and build costs, and anything about how a real speech system sounds.
